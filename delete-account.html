Sprout Android privacy Support

Delete your Sprout account

You can request deletion of your Sprout Android account and its associated data even if you no longer have the app.

Request deletion by email

Email info@eb-net.org from the email address you used for Google sign-in. Ask to delete your Sprout Android account. We may verify that you own the account before processing the request. Please leave passwords, invitation codes and children’s details out of the message.

Email an account deletion request

Delete in the app

Open Settings → Family sharing → Delete Sprout account. You can also choose to erase the local journal and photos on that phone.

What deletion covers

Deletion removes your Sprout sign-in, invites you created, and journals that only you could access. It revokes your membership in shared journals, which stay available to their other members, including entries you added and any author name saved on them. Deleting a Sprout account does not delete your Google account. Copies in your photo gallery or on other devices can be managed separately.
