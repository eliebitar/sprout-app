Getting started

Do I need an account?

No. Sprout has no sign-up. Your journal is stored on your iPhone and synced through your own iCloud account.

How do I log sleep?

Tap the Sleep tile when your child falls asleep and tap it again when they wake up. To add a sleep afterwards, touch and hold the tile and choose “Log Past Sleep”. You can also say “Start a nap in Sprout” to Siri.

How do I take or add photos?

Tap the Photo tile to open the camera. Touch and hold it to choose photos from your library instead. If you allow it, Sprout also shows the photos you took that day so you can keep them with one tap.

Can I log something for an earlier day?

Yes. Use the arrows next to your child's name on the Today screen to go back to any day, then tap a tile.

Kita

How does the arrival reminder work?

In Settings, tap “I'm at Kita Now” while you are at the Kita, then turn on “Ask when I arrive”. When you arrive later, Sprout sends a quiet notification; touch and hold it to log drop-off or pick-up. It always asks first and never logs on its own. Allow location access “Always” for this to work.

How does scanning the daily report work?

Open a Kita day and tap “Scan the daily report”. Sprout reads the text on your iPhone (German and English) and adds it to the notes. If it finds a nap time such as “Schlaf 12:15 – 13:40”, it offers to add the nap with one tap.

Sync and data

How do I use Sprout on two iPhones?

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, on both devices. Sprout syncs automatically through your private iCloud database. Settings → Privacy in Sprout shows whether iCloud sync is on.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual entries in the app (touch and hold an entry, or open it and tap Delete). To remove everything, delete the app, then on your iPhone go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage and delete the Sprout data.

Are the growth percentiles medical advice?

No. The dashed line shows the WHO median for orientation only. Every child grows at their own pace — please talk to your pediatrician about any concerns.
