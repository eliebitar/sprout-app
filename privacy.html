Sprout Overview Support Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

The short version: we collect nothing. Sprout: Baby Journal (“Sprout”) has no account, no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you record about your child stays on your devices and in your own private iCloud storage.

What Sprout stores, and where

The entries you create — sleep, meals, Kita days, growth measurements, notes, photos and your child's name, birthday and photo — are stored:

  • On your device, inside the app.
  • In your private iCloud database, if iCloud is turned on, so your journal syncs between your devices. This database belongs to your Apple Account. Apple encrypts it, and the developer of Sprout cannot access it.

No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Permissions

  • Camera — to take photos of moments, meals and the Kita daily report. Photos are saved only in Sprout (and, if you choose, in your Photos library).
  • Photos — to show the photos you took on a given day so you can keep them in one tap, and to save in-app camera photos to your library if you choose. Photos are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Location (optional) — to recognise when you arrive at the Kita location you saved. This check happens on your iPhone; your location is never stored by Sprout or sent anywhere.
  • Notifications (optional) — for the Kita arrival reminder and the optional evening check-in. All notifications are created on your device.

Text recognition

When you scan a Kita daily report, the text is recognised on your device using Apple's Vision framework. The image and text are not sent anywhere.

Children's privacy

Sprout is used by parents to record information about their own children. Because nothing is collected or transmitted to us, we hold no information about you or your child.

Deleting your data

You can delete any entry in the app. To delete everything, delete the app and remove its data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
