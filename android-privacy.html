Sprout for Android Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

Sprout is a journal for parents and caregivers. You can use it without signing in. Google sign-in is optional and lets you sync journals and share them with family members you invite.

Journal data

Sprout saves the entries you add on your Android device. These can include a child’s name, birthday and sex, care routines and times, growth measurements, milestones, photos, voice notes, and written notes. Android can back up journal records and settings according to your device backup settings.

Sign-in, sync, and sharing

If you choose Google sign-in, Google Firebase Authentication uses your Google account name, email address and account identifier to create and manage your Sprout account. Sprout stores your family journal records, account identifiers, and invitation records in Google Cloud Firestore in Frankfurt, Germany. People you invite who join your journal can read and edit its shared records. Firebase processes technical information, including IP addresses during authentication, to protect the service against abuse.

Earlier builds did not send photo or report image files through Google sharing. If you copy photos to your gallery or share them through another app, that app or service handles those copies under its own settings.

Photos and voice notes in shared journals

In the November 2026 internal test builds, if you use Google family sharing, Sprout can upload photos, thumbnails and voice recordings from a shared journal to Google Cloud Firestore in Frankfurt, Germany. This includes attached photos, child profile photos and daycare report images. Sprout uses these temporary copies to deliver the files to members of that journal.

Each transfer expires after 30 days. Sprout removes the server copy after all intended recipients have downloaded it, or when a family member’s app next runs cleanup after it expires. If no member’s app runs cleanup, the server copy can remain longer. Copies already downloaded to another member’s device stay on that device. If a member is missing a file, their app can request a new transfer from a device that still has it.

Device features

The camera and system photo picker are used when you choose to capture or attach an image. The microphone is used when you choose to record a voice note. Sprout reads daycare reports on your device. You can choose which suggestions to save. Sprout uses local notifications for reminders you enable.

Advertising and analytics

Sprout has no ads and does not use Google Analytics for Firebase.

Children’s information

Sprout is intended for adults caring for children. Adults choose what information they record and whom they invite to share a journal.

Delete your account or data

Use Settings → Family sharing → Delete Sprout account in the Android app, or use the online deletion request. You can also delete individual entries in the app. Account deletion removes your sign-in and journals only you could access. Shared journals remain available to their other members, including entries you added and any author name saved on those entries.
